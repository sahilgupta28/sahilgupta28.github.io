About Me

I help businesses turn product ideas into software that scales, stays secure, and is ready for AI. I started as a PHP developer, writing backend code and learning first-hand what makes systems hold up or fall apart in production. Today, as Tech Lead at Teqnovos, I lead our MERN and Python teams and work as a solution architect on client projects: choosing the architecture and tech stack, estimating cost and timelines honestly, and making sure what we ship is built to last.

Much of my work is in SaaS platforms, fintech and healthtech, where performance, security and compliance matter as much as features. More recently I've been building AI into real products: integrating large language models, designing RAG systems that let AI answer from a company's own data, and creating AI tools that solve practical business problems.

I believe good engineering is mostly about good decisions: build the smallest version that proves value, test it properly with TDD and CI/CD, and scale only what works.
